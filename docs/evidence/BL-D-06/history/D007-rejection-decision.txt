## Cold backlog and the operational timing model

The fresh BL-C maximum source-relative response is **4.199441865980589 s**.
It remains in that run and its conservative 3.2 m/s screen. It is **not an
admitted positive-decision age** in the operational path:

- Host capture origin is recorded before owner tick, not relabelled at dequeue.
- Capture age **0.5 s**, processing **0.1 s**, source command expiry **0.1 s**,
  and two fresh results are independently checked before/after governance.
- Cold/over-budget/invalid results return zero throttle/full brake and reset
  warm-up. Episode/detector-generation reset requires warming again.
- A final policy/controller deadline check overrides a computed positive output.
  Adapter dispatch checks host and simulation expiry; invalid/expired/missing
  tracks never imply clearance, and HOLD/emergency precedence remains intact.

independent actuator watchdog or asynchronous-world driving certificate. The
bounded timing interpretation requires the exclusive synchronous owner and
checked packet dispatch; it cannot be extrapolated to an asynchronous control
loop holding its last throttle during a blocking read.

# Lane training and held-out evaluation

The existing YOLO detector is retained. The first training target is the lane model, using labelled images generated by CARLA. Steering, route legality, frame alignment and stop/resume logic are engineering and calibration problems; training a neural network does not repair those modules.

## Dataset and split

Run the Capture stage in [batch35_baseline.md](batch35_baseline.md). The default collection has 12,000 frames at 640 x 360 from Town10HD_Opt, with 20 exact-frame observations per scene, different roads, weather, car/person distances and roadside negatives. CARLA instance segmentation supplies RoadLine labels (class 24), and the collector saves RGB, raw depth, labelled masks, sequence IDs, camera transforms and separate evaluation ground truth. No hand-labelled external dataset is needed for this first experiment.

Road IDs and whole sequences are assigned to train, validation and test splits. Adjacent frames from a sequence cannot be split across them. Dataset validation checks hashes, shape, label IDs, frame alignment and cross-split road/sequence/image leakage. Open `review.html` and inspect masks and boxes before spending hours on training. Missing labels, low lane-pixel coverage or poor scene diversity must be addressed in the data.

The scenes contain parked actors and repeated views; 12,000 frames are not 12,000 independent scenes. This dataset does not demonstrate generalization to other towns or dynamic pedestrians. It is a controlled first fine-tuning dataset and an offline replay source.

## Keep training data off the demo map

The Town10HD capture is the measurement dataset. Perception, depth analysis and threshold freezing use it, and Evaluate tests on its held-out roads. Fine-tuning on it would train on the map being demonstrated. To train on another town, capture a second dataset; the town is loaded for the capture and Town10HD is restored afterwards:

```powershell
& .\scripts\run_batch35.ps1 -Stage Capture -Map Town05
```

`-Map` accepts Town01–Town07 (optionally `_Opt`), Town10HD and Town10HD_Opt; large maps are not supported. A capture on another town is stored as `training_dataset` in the state file and never replaces the Town10HD `dataset`. The dataset manifest records `map` and `demo_map`. `train_lane.py` refuses a dataset recorded on the demo map unless `--allow-demo-map` (`-AllowDemoMap` in the stage script) is given, and `training.json` records `dataset_map` and `trained_on_demo_map`. Evaluate then measures the candidate on Town10HD's held-out test roads: a cross-town result.

## Train a separate candidate

After Capture succeeds:

```powershell
& .\scripts\run_batch35.ps1 -Stage Train -TrainingHours 4
```

Train uses `training_dataset` when one exists, otherwise `dataset`; the latter requires `-AllowDemoMap`.

The script fine-tunes the canonical ResNet18 LaneUNet from the existing local lane checkpoint, without downloading pretrained weights. RGB/255 preprocessing matches the recovered lane inference contract. It uses cross-entropy plus Dice loss and AdamW, saving the best validation candidate and a separate resumable training checkpoint. The existing `lane_unet_best.pth` remains unchanged.

Training stops at the first of 50 epochs, the requested wall-time budget (checked between epochs) or eight epochs without validation improvement. Four hours is a budget, not a requirement to run for four hours after convergence. Do not train longer solely because a timer is available. Review train/validation loss and lane IoU before changing hyperparameters.

Outputs are in a dated `Group7_batch35_Train_*` folder under Downloads. The candidate is `training/lane_candidate.pth`. `training/batch35/train_lane.py --help` describes direct use and resume. Resume restores model/optimizer state; it does not promise bit-for-bit replay of random sampling. Large datasets/checkpoints are excluded from the patch and should stay out of Git.

## Freeze the choice, then evaluate

Choose settings on the validation split first. Then run:

```powershell
& .\scripts\run_batch35.ps1 -Stage Evaluate
& .\scripts\run_batch35.ps1 -Stage Audit
```

Evaluate compares the unchanged baseline with the candidate on the held-out test roads, writes confusion-derived lane precision/recall/IoU and side-by-side overlay files, and records hashes. Audit separately evaluates the existing YOLO/depth pipeline on held-out sequences. Neither command promotes a model into control.

Test results should be used once for the frozen candidate. If test results guide another training change, that test set is no longer untouched; collect a new held-out set before making a final generalization claim. Only adopt a candidate after reviewing metrics and overlays for ordinary roads, bends, shadows, intersections and failures. Lane output remains HUD-only in this baseline even if segmentation improves.

## Obstacle detector fine-tuning (pedestrians)

The held-out audit (TEST-09, `docs/evidence/TEST-09-heldout-audit/`) found the
registered `yolo26n.pt` misses child-sized pedestrians, including one at 5.3 m, and
does not confirm the 17.5 m person range. `TrainDetector` fine-tunes the detector on
road users captured on another town, and `EvaluateDetector` measures the candidate on
the demo map's validation and test splits.

```powershell
& .\scripts\run_batch35.ps1 -Stage TrainDetector -TrainingHours 3
& .\scripts\run_batch35.ps1 -Stage EvaluateDetector
```

**Labels.** Each frame's labels come from its instance image, using the capture's
visible-pixel rule (at least 16 pixels):
- person, rider (as person), car, truck, bus, motorcycle and bicycle are all labelled;
- every walker model is included, children too;
- the initial model's 80 COCO classes are kept, so the runtime class filter is unchanged.

Labelling every obstacle class stops the model learning to ignore unlabelled vehicles.
Traffic lights and signs are not labelled, so keep the original model for traffic-light
work.

**Training.**
- Runs at 1280 px, the driven detector size, for up to `-TrainingHours`.
- Selects on the training town's own validation split.
- The demo map is refused unless `-AllowDemoMap`.
- The candidate is `detector\detector_candidate.pt`; `training.json` records its hash,
  the label counts and the person-height spread.

**Evaluation.**
- Replays both demo-map splits at the driven detector size and confidence.
  `benchmark_perception.py --candidate` records the candidate's own SHA-256
  (`model_status: CANDIDATE_UNREGISTERED`).
- `compare_detectors.py` then matches the in-path poses one by one against the baseline
  reports (`perception` for validation, `heldout` for test), and lists the poses fixed,
  the poses broken, and those neither model detects.

**Choosing a candidate.** Choose on the validation comparison. The test split was
already read once by the TEST-09 audit, so report its comparison once and do not tune
against it.

A candidate becomes the runtime detector only through the usual stages:
`models.yaml` registration, Perception, Freeze and acceptance. Until then it is
evidence, not the baseline.

## What has been tested here

A one-epoch CPU run on a tiny synthetic dataset completed, including candidate checkpoint writing. This checks that the training program executes; the random fixture, its low IoU and its weights are not accuracy evidence and are not included as a trained deliverable. Useful model quality, CUDA throughput and the full CARLA dataset remain to be measured on the university PC.
